# Tickdo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]. Placeholders in [brackets] must be filled in before publishing. Have this reviewed by a lawyer familiar with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)._

Tickdo ("we") is a medicine reminder app operated by **[LEGAL ENTITY NAME, address]**. This policy explains what information Tickdo handles, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- Your medicines, reminder times, dose history and stock counts stay **on your phone**, in an encrypted database.
- Prescription photos are read **on your phone**. They are never uploaded, and they are deleted as soon as you finish checking them, or after 7 days at the latest.
- You can use Tickdo without an account. If you create one with your mobile number, we store only what is needed to share your doses with a caregiver you invite.
- We do not sell your data, show ads, or send your health information to analytics or advertising companies.
- You can delete everything, on your phone and on our server, from **Settings → Delete all my data**.

## 1. Information stored only on your phone

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Medicine names, strengths, forms, reminder times, how many to take | To remind you at the times you set |
| Dose history (taken, skipped, missed, and when you recorded it) | To show your history |
| Tablets or units on hand | To warn you before you run out |
| Text read from prescription photos, kept with medicines you confirm | So you can compare against your prescription later |
| Your name (as you typed it) and language | To personalise screens |

This data is encrypted on your device. It is not included in Android cloud backups.

**Prescription photos** are processed on your phone using Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. They are not sent to us, to Google, or to anyone else. Each photo is deleted when you finish reviewing the scan, or 7 days after it was taken, whichever comes first.

## 2. Information stored on our server (only if you create an account)

An account is optional. If you sign in with your mobile number, we store the following on servers operated for us by Supabase in India (Mumbai region):

| What | Why | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Your mobile number | To sign you in with a one-time code | Only you |
| The display name you chose | To show your caregiver who you are | You, and the caregiver you invite |
| Your caregiver's mobile number and the invitation status | To connect you | You, and the invited person |
| **If you turn on caregiver sharing:** medicine labels (for example "Metformin 500 mg"), dose times, and whether each dose was taken, skipped or missed, for about 2 days before and after today, kept for at most 7 days | So your caregiver can see your doses and be alerted if a dose is more than 60 minutes late | You, and your accepted caregiver |
| A notification token for the caregiver's phone | To deliver late-dose alerts | Not shown to anyone |

Sending the one-time code by SMS uses **[SMS PROVIDER]**, which receives your mobile number and the code. Late-dose alerts are delivered through Expo's push service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. The alert text says only that a dose is late; it does not include the medicine name.

When you stop caregiver sharing, everything you shared is deleted from our server.

## 3. Payments

Tickdo Plus subscriptions are processed by **Google Play**. We use **RevenueCat** to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app identifier and purchase receipts. It does not receive your phone number, name or health information. We do not see your payment card details.

## 4. What we do not do

- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
- No analytics SDKs. Crash information comes only from Google Play's anonymous Android vitals, which contain no medicine names, scan text or health data.
- No selling or renting of personal data.
- No AI or cloud services read your prescriptions.

## 5. Your choices and rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

- **Delete everything:** Settings → Delete all my data. This removes your data from the phone and, if you have an account, from our server, and we confirm the server deletion.
- **Stop sharing:** Settings → Caregiver sharing → Stop sharing.
- **Turn off scanning:** Settings → Prescription scanning.
- **Access, correction, grievances:** contact our Grievance Officer at **[NAME, EMAIL, ADDRESS]**. We respond within **[30]** days.

## 6. Children

Tickdo is not directed at children under 18. A parent or guardian can use Tickdo to manage a child's medicines on their own phone.

## 7. Medical disclaimer

Tickdo reminds you to take medicines exactly as you enter them. It does not replace a doctor or pharmacist, and it never suggests or changes doses or timings.

We will post changes here and update the date above. For significant changes, we will tell you in the app.
